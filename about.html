About BHCL

A trusted contractor delivering quality construction across Zanzibar and mainland Tanzania.

Who We Are

Built on experience, driven by craft. BHCL Contractor Co. Ltd is a full-service construction company with hands-on expertise across civil, residential, and commercial works. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern engineering to deliver projects on time, on budget, and beyond expectations.

From rural community buildings to commercial premises and critical infrastructure, our team brings precision, safety, and integrity to every site. We are licensed, insured, and committed to excellence in every project we take on.

Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide every project we deliver.

Our Mission

To build safe, durable, and functional spaces that improve the lives of the communities we serve — from small rural buildings to major commercial premises.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted construction partner in Zanzibar and Tanzania — known for quality, honesty, and reliable delivery on every project.

Our Values

Integrity, safety, craftsmanship, and respect for our clients, our workers, and the environments in which we build.
